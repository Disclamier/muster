## DET Assault Brethren
UNIQUE Doctrines
RULE Assault Mastery
You can select the assault doctrine one additional time per battle.
ENH Imperium's Sword
ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only. This model has the following weapon:
Imperium's Sword (Melee): Range Melee, A 6, WS 2+, S 7, AP -3, D 3.
ENH Furious Assault | upgrade
ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY unit only. This unit's melee attacks have [SUSTAINED HITS 1: non-MONSTER/VEHICLE].
STRAT Armour of Contempt | 1
WHEN: Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
EFFECT: Attacks that target your unit have -1 AP until that enemy unit has attacked.
STRAT Gene-wrought Might | 1
WHEN: Fight phase, when a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit is selected to fight.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
EFFECT: Your unit's melee attacks have:
■ [LANCE].
■ Or: If the assault doctrine is active for your unit, [LANCE] and +1 AP.
STRAT Duty in Death | 1
WHEN: Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
EFFECT: When a model in your unit is destroyed, if your unit has not been selected to fight this phase, roll one D6, with +1 to that roll if the assault doctrine is active for your unit:
■ On a 4+, do not remove that model from the battlefield. When your unit has fought, or at the end of the phase (whichever comes first), that model is removed from the battlefield.

## DET Tactical Brethren
UNIQUE Doctrines
RULE Tactical Mastery
You can select the tactical doctrine one additional time per battle.
ENH Laurels of Vigilance
ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only. (Once per battle round, per army) When you use the Fire Overwatch/Heroic Intervention stratagem, if you target a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES BATTLELINE unit within 12" of this model with that stratagem, that use is -1 CP.
ENH Tactical Insight
CAPTAIN model only. The tactical doctrine is active for this unit in addition to any other combat doctrine.
STRAT Armour of Contempt | 1
WHEN: Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
EFFECT: Attacks that target your unit have -1 AP until that enemy unit has attacked.
STRAT Domination Fire | 1
WHEN: Your Shooting phase, when a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit has shot.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
EFFECT: Select one enemy unit hit by those attacks. That enemy unit is suppressed until the start of your next turn:
■ While a unit is suppressed, that unit's attacks have -1 to hit rolls.
STRAT Masterful Tactics | 1
WHEN: Your opponent's Movement phase, when an enemy unit ends a move within 8" of a friendly unengaged ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY/MOUNTED unit.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY/MOUNTED unit.
EFFECT: Your unit can make a normal move of:
■ Up to D6".
■ Or: If the tactical doctrine is active for your unit, up to 6".
