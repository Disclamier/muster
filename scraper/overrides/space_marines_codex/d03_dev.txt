## DET Devastator Brethren
UNIQUE Doctrines
RULE Devastator Mastery
You can select the devastator doctrine one additional time per battle.
ENH Master-forged Firearms
ADEPTUS ASTARTES INFANTRY/MOUNTED model only. This model's ranged attacks (excluding [PSYCHIC] attacks) have +1 A, S, AP and D.
ENH Honour of Vigilance
ADEPTUS ASTARTES model only.
■ This unit's ranged attacks have [LETHAL HITS].
■ If the devastator doctrine is active for this unit, this unit can re-roll advance rolls.
STRAT Armour of Contempt | 1
WHEN: Your opponent's Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when an enemy unit targets a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
EFFECT: Attacks that target your unit have -1 AP until that enemy unit has attacked.
STRAT Storm of Fire | 1
WHEN: Your Shooting phase, when a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit is selected to shoot.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
EFFECT: Your unit's ranged attacks have:
■ [IGNORES COVER].
■ Or: If the devastator doctrine is active for your unit, [IGNORES COVER] and +1 AP.
STRAT Hail of Vengeance | 2
WHEN: Your opponent's Shooting phase, when an enemy unit has shot, if those attacks destroyed a model in a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
TARGET: That ADEPTUS ASTARTES unit.
EFFECT: Your unit shoots using normal shooting, but while doing so your unit can only target that enemy unit.
