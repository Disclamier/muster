## DET Phobos Shadow Force
UNIQUE Phobos
RULE Shadow Masters
When a friendly PHOBOS or SCOUT SQUAD unit has shot:
■ Those attacks do not prevent that unit from being hidden.
■ Or: That unit can make a normal move of up to D6". That unit is not eligible to declare a charge until the end of the turn.
ENH Venator Omni-auspex
PHOBOS model only. This unit's attacks that target a hidden unit can:
■ Re-roll wound rolls of 1.
■ Or: If the assault doctrine is active for this unit, re-roll wound rolls of 1 and 2.
ENH Execute and Redeploy
PHOBOS model only. In your Shooting phase, after this unit has shot, if this unit is unengaged, this unit can make a normal move of up to 6". If it does, this unit is not eligible to declare a charge until the end of the turn.
STRAT Strike from the Shadows | 1
WHEN: Your Shooting phase or the Fight phase, when a friendly hidden PHOBOS unit is selected to attack.
TARGET: That PHOBOS unit.
EFFECT: Your unit's attacks have:
■ +1 S.
■ [LETHAL HITS].
STRAT Mortis Snares | 1
WHEN: End of your Movement phase.
TARGET: One friendly unengaged PHOBOS/SCOUT SQUAD unit.
EFFECT: If your unit is controlling an objective, that objective is snared. While an objective is snared, when an enemy unit ends a move within range of that objective, roll one D6:
■ On a 2+, that enemy unit suffers D6 mortal wounds.
■ That objective is no longer snared.
STRAT Tactical Withdrawal | 1
WHEN: End of your opponent's Fight phase.
TARGET: One friendly unengaged PHOBOS/SCOUT SQUAD unit.
EFFECT: Place your unit in strategic reserves.
