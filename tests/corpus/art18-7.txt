I'm an uppie downie shooty army with charge from 6" DS.

My detachment rule is to re-roll 1s to hit, and to make tunnels:

- The Red Terror and the Mawlocs make a 40mm tunnel marker within 1" when they arrive from DS.

- Other units in reserve can DS wholly within 9" of tunnel markers, and then arrive outside 6" of your units.

- Charging is allowed.

- If on of your units end a move within 3" of a tunnel marker it dies.

My strats are, in order of importance:

- Uppie downie, either both Mawlocs or any one unit

- On board teleport between tunnels

- Sus 1 and ignore cover on unit arriving from reserves

- 6" re-roll charge aura on a monster that arrived from reserves

- Heal/rez d3+1 wholly within 9" of a tunnel

- Synapse on a Mawloc

Important tricks:

- The Red Terror has innate uppie downie if unenganged, on _both_ player's turns

- Swarmy has vect

- Maleceptors have a 6" aura of -1 to hit, also -1 to wound if you are below half strength.

- Neurolictors cause a command phase BS test within 12", and give battle-shocked units within 12" -1 to hit and +1 to be wounded.

- On arriving from DS, Mawlocs roll a d6 for each unit within 12" and deal mortals. 1: nothing, 2-4: d3, 5+: 3 and a BS test.
